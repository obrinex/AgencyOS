# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Obrinex** — Effective Date: 24 July 2026

## 1. General Policy
All payments made to Obrinex — including for consultation, discovery, planning, development, AI configuration, integrations, testing, deployment, and support — are generally **non-refundable**, except as expressly described in this policy.

## 2. Client Cancellation Mid-Project
If a client cancels an active project:
- The 50% upfront payment already made is retained by Obrinex.
- The client is not liable for future milestones that have not yet been reached.
- All completed work, source files, prompts, configurations, and associated intellectual property remain the **exclusive property of Obrinex** unless otherwise agreed in writing. Partial deliverables may be withheld pending resolution.
- This is separate from, and does not affect, the ownership-transfer terms in the Intellectual Property Rights Policy, which apply only to fully completed and paid engagements.

## 3. Cancellation Before Work Begins
If a client cancels before any work has started, a partial refund **may** be issued, solely at Obrinex's discretion, after deducting administrative costs, payment gateway charges, consultation fees, and any expenses already incurred. This is not guaranteed.

## 4. Exceptional Cases
At Obrinex's sole discretion, exceptional circumstances may be resolved through a partial or full refund, complimentary work, additional features, extended maintenance, service credits, discounts, or an alternative solution. No request automatically qualifies for any of these remedies.

## 5. Circumstances That Do Not Qualify for a Refund
Including but not limited to: change of mind or business plans, budget constraints, delays caused by the client, missed meetings, unapproved milestones, third-party service issues, market changes, requests to change already-approved work, lack of technical understanding of the deliverable, or simply not using a delivered service.

## 6. Third-Party Costs
Costs paid to third parties on the client's behalf or at the client's direction — domains, hosting, APIs, AI model usage, WhatsApp Business API, voice/SMS/email providers, payment gateways, licenses — are non-refundable through Obrinex and are governed by those providers' own policies.

## 7. Maintenance Periods
Completed maintenance periods are non-refundable. Future renewals may be cancelled with prior written notice.

## 8. Warranty Interaction
If a client's or an unauthorized third party's involvement causes the warranty to be revoked under the Warranty Policy, no refund is available for maintenance or support fees already paid, regardless of remaining time on the maintenance period.

## 9. Chargebacks
Initiating a chargeback without first contacting Obrinex in good faith may be treated as a breach of contract. Obrinex reserves the right to suspend work, revoke access where legally permissible, terminate services, and pursue recovery of amounts owed.

## 10. Resolution Philosophy
Obrinex's default approach to any dispute is resolution first — revisions, consultation, or restructuring — before financial remedies are considered. Final decisions on refunds rest with Obrinex, subject to applicable law and the dispute resolution process in the Terms and Conditions.

## 11. Policy Updates
This policy may be updated from time to time. The most recently published version supersedes all prior versions.
