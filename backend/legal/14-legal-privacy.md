# Privacy Policy

**Obrinex** — Effective Date: 26 July 2026

## 1. Introduction
This policy explains how Obrinex ("we," "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data in connection with our website, client portal, and AI automation services, and how we comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and its associated Rules.

## 2. What We Collect

**From direct clients (the businesses that contract with us):** name, business name, contact details (email, phone), billing and payment information, project requirements, and communications with our team or AI agents.

**From data processed on a client's behalf:** where a delivered system (a chatbot, CRM, voice agent, or similar) is designed to collect or process personal data belonging to our client's own customers, we process that data strictly as instructed by, and on behalf of, that client, for the duration of the active engagement.

## 3. How We Use Data
To deliver, support, and maintain the services contracted; to communicate with clients about their projects, invoices, and support requests; to improve our own internal tooling; and to meet legal and regulatory obligations.

## 4. Where and How Data Is Stored
Client and project data is stored primarily via MongoDB Atlas, with hosting and infrastructure provided through Vercel, Render, Hostinger, and Google Suite. Reasonable technical and organizational security measures are applied — see the Security Policy for detail.

## 5. Data Handling During and After Engagement
While a client's security and maintenance period is active, Obrinex holds the full data relevant to the delivered system, including data belonging to that client's own customers where applicable. **Once the engagement is properly handed over to the client, that data is removed from Obrinex's systems** — Obrinex thereafter retains only the direct client's own business and account data.

## 6. Sub-processors and Third-Party Services
In the course of delivering services, Obrinex may use the following categories of third-party providers, which may process personal data as sub-processors on our behalf: AI/language model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Nvidia, Groq, OpenRouter), voice and conversational AI platforms (Vapi, ElevenLabs, Voiceflow, Botpress), automation and workflow platforms (n8n, Make.com, Zapier), hosting and infrastructure (MongoDB Atlas, Vercel, Render, Hostinger, Google Suite), communication tools (Resend, Loom), and other specialized tooling used in specific deliverables (including Higgsfield, Utopi AI, BlueDot, Opal, and Apollo, as relevant to a given project).

Some of these providers may process data outside India. Where this occurs, it is carried out consistent with the cross-border transfer provisions of the DPDP Act.

## 7. Data Principal Rights
Under the DPDP Act, individuals whose personal data we process have the right to access information about their data, request correction or erasure, withdraw consent, and file a grievance. Requests can be directed to our Grievance Officer (Section 9).

## 8. Data Retention
Data belonging to a client's own customers is retained only for as long as the relevant engagement's security and maintenance period is active, and is removed upon proper handover. Direct client account and business data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for legal, accounting, and business purposes after the relationship ends.
