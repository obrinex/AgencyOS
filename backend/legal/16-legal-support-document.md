# Support Document

**Obrinex** — Effective Date: 26 July 2026

## 1. Support Channels
The primary support channel is the AI support chatbot inside each client's dashboard portal. Email support is available at support@obrinex.space as a secondary channel. Additional channels (WhatsApp, voice, and others) are planned for future rollout and are not yet live — clients will be notified when they become available.

## 2. Support Hours
Monday–Friday, 09:00–21:00 IST. Saturday and Sunday are closed, though monitoring continues on a best-effort basis outside these hours.

## 3. Response and Resolution Targets
- First response: 30 minutes to 24 hours, depending on volume and complexity.
- Simple issues: 30 minutes–24 hours.
- Moderate issues: 2–7 business days.
- Medium-complex issues: 7–14 business days.
- Complex or major issues: handled case-by-case, with regular progress updates.

All clients receive the same treatment under these targets — there is no separate priority tier.

## 4. What's Included in Maintenance
Routine health checks, bug monitoring and minor fixes, security maintenance, performance optimization, compatibility updates, and general technical support, for as long as the client's maintenance plan is active.

## 5. What's Not Included
Issues caused by third-party outages, global service disruptions, or force majeure events are outside our control and are addressed on a best-effort basis rather than under standard resolution targets.

## 6. Warranty Interaction — Important
Support and warranty coverage depend on the maintenance plan remaining active and untampered. **If any unauthorized third party modifies, accesses, or integrates with a delivered system during the maintenance period, the warranty is permanently revoked, and no further maintenance services will be provided — even if the maintenance period is otherwise still valid and paid for.** See the Warranty Policy for full detail.

## 7. After the Mandatory First-Year Period
Once the mandatory first-year maintenance period ends, further security and maintenance work is available on a one-time, flat-fee basis per instance, rather than as an ongoing subscription, unless the client chooses to continue a maintenance plan.

## 8. Escalation
If a support issue cannot be resolved through the standard process, or touches on billing, legal, or security matters, it is escalated internally for direct review before any resolution is communicated to the client.

## 9. Maintenance Windows
Scheduled maintenance may cause partial interruptions of up to 24–48 hours. Advance notice is given where reasonably possible.
