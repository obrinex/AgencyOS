# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Obrinex** — Effective Date: 26 July 2026

This DPA applies where Obrinex processes personal data on behalf of a client in the course of delivering a system (such as a CRM, chatbot, or voice agent) that collects or processes personal data belonging to that client's own customers. It supplements the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy.

## 1. Roles
For the purposes of this DPA, the client is the data fiduciary (controller) determining the purpose of processing, and Obrinex acts as data processor, processing personal data only on the client's documented instructions and for the purpose of delivering and maintaining the contracted system.

## 2. Scope and Duration of Processing
Processing occurs only for the duration of the active engagement, specifically the period during which the client's security and maintenance plan is active. Upon proper handover of the delivered system to the client, Obrinex removes the relevant personal data from its own systems and retains only the direct client's own account and business data.

## 3. Sub-processors
The client authorizes Obrinex to engage the following categories of sub-processors in the course of delivering services:

- **AI/language model providers:** OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Nvidia, Groq, OpenRouter
- **Voice and conversational AI platforms:** Vapi, ElevenLabs, Voiceflow, Botpress
- **Automation and workflow platforms:** n8n, Make.com, Zapier
- **Hosting and infrastructure:** MongoDB Atlas, Vercel, Render, Hostinger, Google Suite
- **Communication tools:** Resend, Loom
- **Other specialized tooling as relevant to a given project:** Higgsfield, Utopi AI, BlueDot, Opal, Apollo

Obrinex remains responsible for ensuring sub-processors provide an adequate level of protection for any personal data they process.

## 4. Security Measures
Obrinex implements reasonable technical and organizational measures as described in the Security Policy, including access controls, encryption in transit, regular backups (every 7 days), and defined incident response procedures.

## 5. Breach Notification
In the event of a personal data breach affecting data processed under this DPA, Obrinex will notify the client within **48 hours** of becoming aware of the breach, to enable the client to meet its own obligations as data fiduciary, including any notifications required under the DPDP Act.

## 6. Audit Rights
The client has **limited audit rights** under this DPA: the client may request, no more than once per year, a written summary of Obrinex's relevant security practices. This DPA does not provide for on-site audits or unrestricted system access, given the scale of Obrinex's operations.

## 7. Data Deletion and Return
Upon completion or termination of the engagement and proper handover, Obrinex will delete personal data processed on the client's behalf from its own systems, except where retention is required by law.

## 8. Client Obligations
The client warrants that it has a lawful basis under the DPDP Act for any personal data it instructs Obrinex to process, and that its instructions to Obrinex comply with applicable law.

## 9. Liability
Liability under this DPA is subject to the cap set out in the Terms and Conditions.

## 10. Changes
This DPA may be updated from time to time, including to reflect changes in sub-processors or applicable law. Material changes will be communicated to active clients.
