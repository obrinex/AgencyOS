# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Obrinex** — Effective Date: 26 July 2026

## 1. Support Hours
Monday–Friday, 09:00–21:00 IST. Weekends closed, with best-effort monitoring only.

## 2. Response and Resolution Targets
- First response: 30 minutes–24 hours.
- Simple issues: 30 minutes–24 hours.
- Moderate issues: 2–7 business days.
- Medium-complex issues: 7–14 business days.
- Complex/major issues: case-dependent, with regular progress updates.

These targets apply uniformly to all clients.

## 3. Uptime
For systems hosted or operated by Obrinex, uptime is provided on a **best-effort basis**. Given reliance on third-party hosting and infrastructure providers (including Vercel, Render, Hostinger, and MongoDB Atlas), no fixed uptime percentage is guaranteed.

## 4. Scheduled Maintenance
Scheduled maintenance may cause partial interruptions of up to 24–48 hours. Advance notice will be given where reasonably possible.

## 5. Backups
Data backups are performed every 7 days.

## 6. Guarantees
Obrinex guarantees: quality workmanship built to agreed scope and tested against best practices; timely communication; transparency; privacy and confidentiality of client information; and continuous improvement of delivered systems. **Obrinex does not guarantee specific business outcomes**, including revenue, leads, rankings, or similar results.

## 7. Warranty and Maintenance
Warranty coverage remains active only while a maintenance plan is active, and is governed in full by the Warranty Policy — including that unauthorized third-party involvement permanently revokes warranty and future maintenance eligibility, regardless of remaining maintenance period.

## 8. Security Incidents
In the event of a security incident affecting a client's system or data, Obrinex will notify the affected client within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

## 9. Exclusions
This SLA does not cover issues arising from third-party service outages, global infrastructure disruptions, force majeure events, or unauthorized modifications made by the client or any third party.

## 10. Changes
This SLA may be updated from time to time. The most recently published version applies.
