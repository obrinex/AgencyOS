# Intellectual Property Rights Policy

**Obrinex** — Effective Date: 26 July 2026

## 1. Ownership During the Maintenance Period
Upon delivery, the client receives a **license to use** the delivered system for the duration of the mandatory first-year security and maintenance period. Full ownership has not yet transferred during this period.

## 2. Ownership Transfer
Once the mandatory first-year maintenance period is completed — and provided all applicable fees have been paid and the engagement has not been affected by any warranty-revoking event (see the Warranty Policy) — **full ownership of the delivered system transfers to the client.**

## 3. Obrinex's Retained IP
Obrinex retains ownership of its own pre-existing and independently developed intellectual property — including proprietary frameworks, reusable code, prompt libraries, internal tooling, and methodologies — that may underlie or be used in the course of building a client's delivered system. Ownership transfer under Section 2 applies to the client's specific deliverable, configuration, and data — not to Obrinex's underlying reusable technology, which Obrinex may continue to use and reuse across other client engagements.

## 4. Third-Party and Open-Source Components
Deliverables may incorporate third-party or open-source software components, which remain subject to their own applicable licenses. Obrinex will use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure such components are appropriately licensed for the client's use.

## 5. Cancelled or Incomplete Engagements
Where an engagement is cancelled before completion, ownership does not transfer, and all completed work, source files, and associated IP remain the exclusive property of Obrinex, in accordance with the Refund and Cancellation Policy.

## 6. Trademarks
Obrinex's name, logo, and branding remain the exclusive property of Obrinex and may not be used by a client without prior written permission, except to accurately identify Obrinex as the developer of a delivered system where relevant.

## 7. Client-Provided Materials
Any content, data, branding, or materials provided by the client for use in a delivered system remain the client's property, and the client warrants it has the right to provide them for that use.

## 8. Changes
This policy may be updated from time to time. The most recently published version applies to engagements entered into after the update.
