# Warranty Policy

**Obrinex** — Effective Date: 26 July 2026

## 1. What's Covered
Obrinex warrants that delivered systems will be free from defects caused by our own implementation, and will fix such defects at no additional cost, for as long as the client's security and maintenance plan remains active.

## 2. Warranty Duration
The warranty period is tied directly to the client's active maintenance plan — it lasts for as long as maintenance is active, not a separate fixed number of days. For clients who take only the mandatory first-year maintenance period, warranty coverage runs for that year.

## 3. After the Mandatory Period
Once the mandatory first-year maintenance period ends, if a client wants further security or maintenance work, it is available on a **one-time flat fee per instance**, rather than as an ongoing warranty or subscription — unless the client chooses to continue an active maintenance plan.

## 4. Warranty Revocation — Third-Party Involvement
**If any unauthorized third party modifies, accesses, integrates with, or otherwise interferes with a delivered system during the maintenance period, the warranty is permanently revoked.** No further maintenance services will be provided from that point forward, even if the maintenance period is otherwise still valid and paid for. This applies regardless of whether the third-party involvement caused the specific issue being reported.

## 5. What's Excluded
The warranty does not cover: issues arising from the client's own misuse or modification of the system; issues caused by unauthorized third-party access or changes (see Section 4); issues arising from third-party services, infrastructure, or outages outside Obrinex's control; or force majeure events.

## 6. How to Claim
Warranty claims should be raised through the standard support channel described in the Support Document, with a description of the issue and when it was first noticed.

## 7. Interaction with Other Policies
This policy operates alongside the Refund and Cancellation Policy, the Service Level Agreement, and the Intellectual Property Rights Policy. A warranty-revoking event may also affect refund eligibility and the conditions for full ownership transfer.

## 8. Changes
This policy may be updated from time to time. The most recently published version applies to active engagements going forward.
